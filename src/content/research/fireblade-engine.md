## Project material, not an external investigation

This note describes Vale's planned Fireblade Engine workflow. It does not make claims about external tools, benchmarks, or research findings.

## The question

Can a structured canon, reusable asset catalog, sequence manifest, and panel-level revision model make graphic-novel production more coherent and repeatable?

## Why this matters

Fireblade is the current build priority in Vale's compounding queue. The intended reusable output is a sequence and asset engine that can inform later multimedia work.

## What this does not show

The workflow is a concept until it produces a complete readable volume and a new sequence can be generated from structured inputs without panel-by-panel babysitting.

## Next experiment

Create one complete sequence using structured canon, visual references, an asset catalog, and a revision log; record where continuity or review fails.
