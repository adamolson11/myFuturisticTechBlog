## Exploratory concept, not a factual article

Dream Forensics is a Vale/Wolves story and research seed. It must not be read as a claim that current technology can faithfully record or replay dreams.

## The question

What fidelity can mental-imagery or dream-content decoding support, and how can a system show the difference between recovered signal and details supplied by a generative model?

## Why this matters

The uncertainty is the premise: an authoritative-looking reconstruction could contain model priors rather than recovered memory, and viewing it could affect later recollection.

## Strongest skeptical case

Plausible generated imagery is not evidence of faithful measurement. Any future investigation must examine controls, sample size, replication, cross-subject performance, and the risk of generative-prior contamination.

## Next experiment

Start with a research packet that separates demonstrated mental-imagery decoding results from speculative narrative implications. No human-subject data collection is proposed here.
