## Project summary

Fireblade is Vale's current build priority. Its exit condition is a complete readable Volume 1 draft and a workflow capable of producing a new sequence from structured inputs without panel-by-panel babysitting.

## Intended reusable outputs

- Structured canon and source authority
- Asset catalog and reference locking
- Sequence manifests and panel-level revisions
- Visual QA and batch generation/review workflows
